# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Participants in this project are expected to behave professionally and respectfully.
Disagreement about technical decisions is welcome; personal attacks, harassment, and
discrimination are not.

## Expected behavior

- Critique the code, the design, and the evidence — not the person.
- Assume good faith and state the constraints you are working under.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change for scope reasons without it being a
  judgement of your competence.
- Keep discussions on topic; move long tangents to a separate issue.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Discriminatory comments about identity, background, or experience level.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberately submitting code that hides cost, hides failures, or weakens a security
  control while claiming otherwise.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any project space, and to
representatives of the project acting in public.

## Enforcement

Report incidents to the maintainers at the contact address in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may warn, remove content, or ban a participant,
and will explain the decision to the reporter. Maintainers who are themselves involved in
an incident must recuse themselves from handling it.

## Attribution

This policy is aligned with the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1. Where this document is less specific, the Contributor Covenant applies.
